Follow the contribution

From contribution
to care.

A contribution needs a continuous trail. These four steps form the tracking logic proposed by this platform.

01

Receipt

Record the source of funds, timing, contribution method, and total value received.

02

Allocation

Publish the recipient organization, intended purpose, approved budget, and relevant agreements.

03

Disbursement

Reconcile transfer documents, confirm receipt, and update disbursement status.

04

Impact Report

Update patient numbers, services deployed, and results provided by partners, subject to verification.

Independent verification matters.

Do not self-label internal data as "audited". Only display an audited status when an official report is available, clearly stating the auditing firm, reporting period, scope, and conclusion.

Our accountability commitments

Trust is a practice.

01

Clear Source & Destination

Distinguish between public donations, corporate sponsorships, in-kind gifts, event revenue, and actual funds transferred to beneficiaries.

02

Scheduled Updates

Publish quarterly and annual reports; clearly state the update date, data period, and any adjustments made.

03

Patient Protection

Only publish aggregated data or explicitly consented data; never display identifying information of patients.
